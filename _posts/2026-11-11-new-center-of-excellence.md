The [University of Vermont](https://www.uvm.edu/) is launching a new [Center of Excellence in Modeling Complexity](https://vermontcomplexsystems.org/research/modeling-complexity/).
The Center will, under my direction, advance predictive modeling of systems shaping health, society, the economy, technology, and the environment in the [Vermont Complex Systems Institute](https://vermontcomplexsystems.org/)

Our objective is to integrate modeling efforts from the structure of societal problems (risk assessment and network/spreading dynamics) to development of solutions (diffusion of innovations and governance models).
